The scriptures teach us that great things will be taught out of the mouth of babes (see [Matthew 21:16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/21?lang=eng&id=p16#p16)). 

One day, my wife had the following conversation with our then five-year-old son as the world was preparing for the Winter Olympics:

> Mom: "The Olympics are coming soon!"  
> Son: "What are the Olympics?"  
> Mom: "It's a big event where they have races and competitions."  
> Son: "So all the fastest people go there?"  
> Mom: "Yeah."  
> (*subdued*) Son: "Where is it?"  
> Mom: "It's in Italy this year."  
> (*subdued and slowly*) Son: "How come they didn't tell me?"

When our son viewed the whole world, and considered all the fastest people in it, he concluded that he belonged in that group. It seems he was thinking: *Surely if they gather all the fastest people in the world they would tell me!* 

There is a lesson here in believing in yourself and believing you belong. 

Are you part of a group that includes special individuals that get invitations to special places? You are. That group is the children of God. That special place is the Celestial Kingdom, the place where God lives. All are invited, and the way is made possible through Jesus Christ.

How many people are part of this special group? All of us. Every person on earth is a child of God (see [Psalm 82:6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ps/82?lang=eng&id=p6#p6), [Acts 17:28](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/acts/17?lang=eng&id=p28#p28), [Romans 8:16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/rom/8?lang=eng&id=p16#p16), [Moses 1:13](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/1?lang=eng&id=p13#p13)). Each of us is a member of that precious group. 

There is a place for the children of God. There are many who do not know they are part of this group. Whose duty is it to tell them?

How many of these friends will stand at judgment day and, through no fault of their own, be left to ask, *"How come they didn't tell me?"*

It is our duty. The Lord has called his laborers (see [Jacob 5:61](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/jacob/5?lang=eng&id=p61#p61)) in these last days. It is the final time. We are to be telling our families, friends, and neighbors that they too are invited to come. 

Do you believe you are part of this group? Are you included in the invitation? You are! You have not been left out. Come!

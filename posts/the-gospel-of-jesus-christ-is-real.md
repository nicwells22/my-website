It is easy for me to get excited about the Gospel of Jesus Christ. It is a gift I have been blessed with by God. As we go through our life, it can be so easy to forget how real this all is.

I had the opportunity to teach a class this morning which included a portion about the millennium and Jesus Christ’s reign on the earth. That will be such an exciting time. The time we live in now preparing for that time is also an exciting time to live in. One reason it is exciting is because it is real!

- The Second Coming of Jesus Christ is really a thing.
- The Millennial reign of Jesus Christ is really a thing.
- The Priesthood, God’s power and authority here on the earth, is really a thing.
- The prophecies of the prophets are real and will come to pass.

Oh how I love the Gospel of Jesus Christ!
